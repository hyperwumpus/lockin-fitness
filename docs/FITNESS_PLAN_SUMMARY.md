# Fitness Plan Summary — Phase 1

## Goal
Start at 190 lb and reduce body fat while rebuilding muscle, strength and work capacity. Approximate scale target: 160 lb, but athletic performance and body composition take priority over forcing a specific number.

## Starting nutrition targets
- ~2,100 kcal/day average
- ~160 g protein/day
- target trend: ~0.5–1 lb/week
- carbs remain in the plan to support training

## No-decision food structure
- Breakfast: protein shake providing ~40–50 g protein + banana.
- Post-training lunch: ~6 oz chicken/tilapia + ~1 cup rice + vegetables.
- Emergency: premade protein shake.
- Dinner: ~6 oz chicken/tilapia + ¾–1 cup rice + vegetables.
- Lazy backup: tuna + corn + portioned rice.
- Planned sweet: high-protein Greek yogurt + fruit; small measured topping if desired.
- Saturday: one flexible meal, not a cheat day.
- Sunday: food restock + rice/prep reset.

## Training Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4
- Monday: Full Body A
- Tuesday: Porter Park Ninja Foundation
- Wednesday: Full Body B
- Thursday: Porter Park Ninja Foundation
- Friday: Full Body A
- Saturday: optional fun movement
- Sunday: recovery/reset
- Next week alternates B/A/B.

Weeks 1–2: 2 working sets on most strength movements, 8–12 reps, leave ~2–3 good reps in reserve.
Weeks 3–4: progress most movements to 3 sets if form/recovery are good.

## Full Body A
1. Squat
2. Push
3. Row
4. Romanian deadlift
5. Assisted pull-up / pulldown
6. Plank

## Full Body B
1. Leg press / goblet squat
2. Incline press
3. Seated / DB row
4. Split squat
5. Assisted pull-up / pulldown
6. Dead bug

## Minimum Mission
3 rounds:
- 10 bodyweight squats
- 5–10 push-ups
- 10 dumbbell rows/side
- 10 reverse lunges total
- 20-second plank

## Porter Park Ninja Foundation
5 min brisk walk → 5–10 min easy jump rope → 5–10 min balance/coordination/mobility → 5 min walk home.

## Later skill tree
Pull-ups, handstand, L-sit, pistol squat, sprinting, jumping, agility, grip/climbing and other athletic “ninja” skills are layered in after the base is rebuilt.
